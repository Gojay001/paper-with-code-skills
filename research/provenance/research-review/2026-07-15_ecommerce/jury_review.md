## Strongest objections

- SKU-TraceBench may be benchmark inflation unless it proves SKU-version-level hard-negative discrimination and human publishability calibration.
- Evidence-Bounded Camera overlaps multi-view identity work unless it learns calibrated risk, reports risk-coverage and supports abstention.
- Risk-Routed Product Lock resembles object-insertion pipelines unless the routing policy is learned and generalizes across SKU.
- Product-Frequency Router is too close to ConsisID-style frequency decomposition; archived pending causal evidence.

These objections were incorporated into the report, research contract and experiment gates.
